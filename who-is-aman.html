Our mission

Safe minds, strong faith, healthy futures

Aman Institute promotes the intellectual, spiritual, and mental safety of young people and provides solutions to the deviant thinking that leads to addiction, violence, extremism, and life failure.

We work with youth, parents, and the wider community through counseling, workshops, parenting support, research, and online programs such as Tanweer, grounded in a published series of studies on intellectual security for the Muslim community in America.

أمان

Why “Aman”?

Aman is Arabic for safety, security, and peace of mind. It names what we hope every young person can have: a mind that is protected, a faith that is secure, and a future that is safe.

Intellectual security

What we mean by intellectual security

  • It is an integral part of the overall security of society, alongside other kinds of security.
  • It rests on preserving a person's identity and thinking from deviation, and on holding to the moderation of Islam.
  • It stems from the Holy Qur'an and the purified Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Flexibility and renewal are characteristics of Islamic thought.

Why it matters

Good for the individual, the family, and society

  • The individual

    Intellectual security protects the mind from deviant ideology and misguided thinking, so young people can think clearly and choose well.

  • The family

    Families built on a sound foundation, enjoying social and intellectual security, are the building blocks of a strong and healthy society.

  • The community

    It preserves a community's identity and helps detect deviant ideologies before they take root.
