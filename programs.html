Tanweer online program

{% include icon.html name="video" class="size-4" %}Online on Zoom {% include icon.html name="lightbulb" class="size-4" %}“Tanweer” means enlightenment

Youth and the threats to which remedies are required.

Tanweer is an online program on the pressures facing young people today, exploring remedies rooted in faith, family, and sound thinking. Its sessions cover topics from smoking and the dark web to depression, extremism, and parenting.
